Early Life

Mukhopadhyay (sometimes erroneously shortened to Mukherjee) has been nothing but maximally delightful since the moment her (now oft-toto trodden) foot made contact with home soil. Uncannily observant from a an age most babies can only goo-goo and ga-ga (ooh la la), her inherent flair for worldbuilding and dreaming up hordes of all-too-real characters made her the kind of storyteller that even Enid Blyton (read: Gnid) would rightly be in awe of.

Mukhopadhyay knew who she was from an early age (as evidenced by images of her junior-self). At barely seven, she once stood toe to toe with actor Shah Rukh Khan in a tasteful yellow frock with her hands on her hips, exuding a sense of confidence and self-possession that he could only ever hope to possess.

Even during her earliest days at school, Mukhopadhyay could be found calming ears with her golden pipes, or sniffing around for Agatha Christie and the like in the library, or being doted over by teachers enamoured with her work, or drawing up a storm of sceneries, characters and comics with a singular ball-point pen in her six-subject notebook (and sometimes everything, everywhere, all at once). At lunch, wherever her silverfoil-wrapped cubes of Amul Cheese were was where she was, with SROSRY close by.

An uneventful day was like light travelling linearly near a black hole with her around. From MU👁WWH, to counting the exact number of hall air-conditioners and stacked chairs seated a mere twenty yards away from Ruskin Bond, to her head being the ideal bouncy-spot for yellow SmileyTM balls, to authoring the wackiest poems known to mankind with The Marauders, each moment was an unforgettable delight.

Immensely lurved by all having the privilege of knowing her, Meghna "Meg" Mukhopadhyay is guaranteed to be the best person you will ever meet. This is a foundational truth of The Universe. Periodt.

Personality

When you see her for the first time, thoughts like, "She looks so serious," and "I can't go talk to her, she is so cool" might cross your mind, but the only correct course of action is to rid yourself of these fears and march straight up to her with a "VILL YOU VEAR VIGS?", for then, and only then will the best days of your life begin, gallivanting across the 42 planes/plains of Life, the Universe and Everything with grins the size of Jupiter's moons. Her name ends in YAY, afterall.

Moony is awesome beyond words; she is sunshine and sparkles personified. Time is not real when you spend your days with her - she is so endlessly entertaining that an hour in her company feels like a minute. Before you know it, you will have laughed yourselves into the next day together.

She has graciously carried this innate charm, and it has only amplified and grown since, all to encompass a diverse potpourri of talents. Writing, singing, simultaneously playing the ukulele, painting, free-sketching comics, crocheting (note: curse string tension), punnery, coding: she has done it all, joyfully and magnificently. It is hard to tell what ties these wondrous character-strings to Mukhopadhyay's personality ship - perhaps it was the perennial pixie cut (now a flourishing flowing head of curl waterfall), or the fascinatingly geometrical cranium that sealed the deal - for it has been mysteriously left entirely to the speculation of the masses.

Moony will stay up to listen to all your psychic moans and groans for your end-of-the-day debrief and offer up the cheeriest solutions to everything that ails you. No conversation is too wild and no tangent too off-topic. With her, you might be discussing why birds aren't aeroplanes one moment, and contemplating the strange memory vacuum Sherlock episodes disappear into the next, each digression becoming more intriguing than the last.

There has never been a sman quite as smanny as Sman, and there never shall be. This Sman IS (NOT) IMMORTAL. This Sman is Best Ferg on Little Green Lilypad in a ripple-y Lake full of hyacinths and a gentle breeze.

Abilities

Endless and fabulously terrific.

Where would we be without Moony to sing every song under the sun with? In a mind-made ditch twelve thousand feet in the ground. One of the greatest joys to have experienced in life is to have hollered every Moana, La La Land and Tangled song in every classroom imaginable with individual in question. A formative experience unlike any other.

Moony crochets like the free end of the wool is on fire and she is being chased by a horde of train-robbers in the desert. Is somehow capable of completing entire articles of clothing in mere hours (so long, CTS). Creates likenesses of talented famous people with but her simple blue ball-point quill so real that they might just jump off the page. Paints bicycles so alive they seem to pedal past on the canvas.

Has the magical ability of conjuring up comic masterpieces both visual and auditory at will and on demand. Impressively has no truck with death (so long, Neruda).

Somehow also insanely good at yoga (further reinforced by positive reinforcement in the form of egg rolls). Moony is capable of flattening enemies into pancakes with her strength training and inexplicable ability to lift both legs whilst having one being weighed down intentionally. Borderline psychopathic.

The computer is a spell and Mukhopadhyay is Gandalf. How else would anyone be able to cook up code hexes and understand networks and backends and databases AND be able to explain such concepts to muggly beings?

The duality of Moony is a befuddler. Will burn water, and cook up scrumptious looking pan-made chocolate cake in the same day. Will happily attempt to feed you multiple crispy chicken burgers should you arrive as a houseguest whilst attempting to retrieve a million bottles of Coke from hive-mind delivery drivers.

Relationships

The Marauders

An institution that predates agriculture and settlements and social conventions. Moony has been the foundational bedrock of the insanity that keeps the spirit of the Marauders alive in the hearts of mankind. Wading through the weeds of life alongside fellow Marauders Wormtail, Padfoot, and Prongs since yore, she has brought forth exactly the sort of confusing "bloogleumping" that shall allow this elite committee of clinically insane people to continue being just as elite and just as insane for the years to come.

Tea

Where there is Tea, there is Moony. If the world is against Tea, Moony is against the world. If there is no tea, Moony will climb mountains high, cross oceans deep, storm tea-garden high command, and grow and pick the leaves herself. Bonus points if Sandhya pishi is in-house to make ginger-spiced tea.

The Chapel of St. Thomas The Engineer Engine

A relationship forged in fire from the days of The Giant T-cross. The Chapel has given to her the gifts of Lonkabaaj Fred, Bagwala, and Viscount Mastermind, amongst others.
